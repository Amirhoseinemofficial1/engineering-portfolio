## Selected systems

### Persian Mizaj Intelligence

A seven-stage Persian NLP program spanning OCR and corpus refinement, tokenizer research, model sizing, random-initialized pretraining, grounded retrieval, instruction tuning, and narrow paraphrase repair. No external pretrained weights are used.

**Evidence:** 2.9M parameters, 4,101 indexed records, recorded recall@5/@10 of 1.0 on the narrow Stage 5 evaluation, and 4/4 Stage 7 raw-generation checks. The repository explicitly records that broad generalization and clinical validity are not established.

### Traditional Medicine Body Atlas

A multilingual React/Three.js anatomy system that unifies male and female layers, supports search and structure isolation, and captures pain directly on visible 3D meshes.

**Evidence:** production build passed; unified-female, inline-pain, description, and smoke gates passed; 5,520 female structures and 5,362 male entries are represented. Upstream anatomy licences and the lack of blanket commercial clearance are documented.

### Fundamental Intelligence Brain

A multi-asset intelligence system for collecting material events, preserving evidence, separating fast ingestion from expensive analysis, and controlling adaptive models through validation and rollback gates.

**Work:** event ingestion, source normalization, impact routing, evidence-backed signals, stateful processing, deployment controls, and service observability.

### CEX Hunter and Token RR Intelligence

Venue-aware token research around exact-market evidence, security gates, first-touch outcomes, calibration, provider cooldowns, and fail-closed model promotion.

**Work:** exchange integrations, structured market evidence, token safety, outcome labeling, OOS controls, and professional signal documents.

### Persian Information Retrieval Lab

A hybrid Persian search pipeline using BM25, word TF-IDF, character TF-IDF, reciprocal-rank fusion, and feature-based reranking to return five unique documents per query.

### ArmGuard Predictive Maintenance

A robotic-arm failure classifier using sensor-aware feature engineering, median-imputed pipelines, stratified cross-validation, an XGBoost/LightGBM/Extra Trees ensemble, and F2-driven threshold selection.

### NooraCare Medical Web Platform

A multilingual Next.js 16 and React 19 health-platform interface with guided AI-visit UX, doctor discovery, service and product routes, home visits, orders, and locale-aware RTL behavior. The repository clearly separates product UX from unimplemented clinical and backend claims.

### NFT Hunter

A multi-chain NFT intelligence scanner combining provider health, quality vetoes, wallet and collection evidence, explainable scoring, and verified outcome labels.

### GamePulse and GamePlus

Full-stack game discovery and community products built around Django APIs and React/Vite interfaces, with content workflows, profiles, groups, tournaments, and media-oriented experiences.

### Quantitative Research Systems

A consolidated family of market-data, signal, backtesting, order-book, risk-control, arbitrage, and multi-venue execution systems. Related snapshots are kept in canonical development lines rather than inflated into duplicate projects.

## Working principles

- Claims follow evidence: a passing process is not automatically a correct result.
- Secrets, runtime state, copyrighted corpora, and restricted datasets stay outside source control.
- Models earn promotion through evaluation, not intuition.
- Expensive intelligence is isolated from real-time ingestion.
- Historical records use recoverable file evidence; imported work is never presented as original authorship.
- Research, prototype, local validation, deployment, and production evidence are labeled separately.
